MILESTONES OF SERVICE

Growing Together Since 2017

The first Let Us Dream conference was launched in 2017 to inspire purposeful community volunteerism. From that initial university gathering, all conferences organized have provided a welcoming platform to learn, share, and celebrate selfless acts of community building.

2017 • The First Community Conference
Inaugural gathering held at Christ University, Bangalore, bringing together student volunteers and local NGO leaders.
2020 • 1st Virtual Triennial International Conference
Convened virtually during global pandemic challenges—engaging over 18,000 registrants and 40,000 viewers worldwide.
2023 • 2nd In-Person Triennial International Conference
Hosted on the Bangalore campus with international scholars, community leaders, and delegates from 12+ nations.
2026 • 3rd Triennial International Conference
Theme: Local Wisdom, Global Influence. Scheduled for November 19–21, 2026 in Bangalore.
