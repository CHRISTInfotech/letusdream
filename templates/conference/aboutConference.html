OUR ROOTS & EXPANSION

History of LUD Conferences

The first Let Us Dream conference was launched in 2017 to inspire purposeful community volunteerism. From that initial university gathering, all conferences organized have provided a welcoming platform to learn, share, and celebrate selfless acts of community building.

In 2020, amidst global pandemic challenges, the first Triennial International Conference was conducted virtually—bringing together over 18,000 registrants and 40,000 viewers worldwide.

Today, conferences are held across multiple chapters including India, the United States, Germany, and beyond, engaging diverse generations in intergenerational community progress.
