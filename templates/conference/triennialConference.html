NARRATIVE OF PURPOSE

Our History & Growth

Let Us Dream began with a simple yet powerful idea: those who dream for others create lasting change. In Monroe, Louisiana, everyday stories of hope reignited Fr. Lijo Thomas's vision during a demanding phase of community research.

The first Let Us Dream Conference launched on October 21, 2017, at Louisiana Tech University with under 100 participants and 15 service organizations. By 2019, the model expanded internationally to Binghamton University, Christ University Bangalore, and Trivandrum.

The 2023 Triennial Conference marked our joyous return to in-person global gatherings, uniting scholars, youth innovators, and grassroots NGOs for shared social impact.
