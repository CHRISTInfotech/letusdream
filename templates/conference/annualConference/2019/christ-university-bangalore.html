Community Improvement Dialogues

The LET US DREAM annual community conference at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru was held on November 16, 2019, under the eminent chairperson Mr. David S.R.

The conference promoted networking between volunteers, philanthropists, and community organizations, featuring 3 separate panel discussions in Education, Health, and Social Service.

Collaborative dialogues between NGOs, academics, and civic volunteers
Three expert panel streams: Health, Education, and Social Service
Organizational tables showcasing scalable grassroots initiatives
