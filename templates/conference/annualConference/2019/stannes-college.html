LEADERSHIP & EXPERTISE

Distinguished Conference Chairs

Meet the educators, leaders, and social practitioners who guided the conference dialogue.

Jimmy Cherian

President & Managing Trustee, Creating Futures

Jimmy Cherian is the President and Managing Trustee of Creating Futures, an NGO focused on skills development.

Read biographyClose biography+
Jimmy Cherian is the President and Managing Trustee of Creating Futures, an NGO focused on skills development. He has been instrumental in the conceptualization and setting up of Creating Futures.

Prior to devoting his time completely to Creating Futures, Jimmy held various leadership roles in companies such as Volvo Group, Ciber, CapGemini, HP, and so on. His last position was as Global Head and Global Director of Emerging Technologies at Volvo IT.

He is a leadership and life skills coach, corporate trainer, and facilitator actively involved in the social development sector across Education, Child Welfare, and Skill Development.

Priya Krishnamurthy

Co-founder & CEO, CMCA (Children's Movement for Civic Awareness)

Priya Krishnamurthy is co-founder and CEO of CMCA - Children’s Movement for Civic Awareness.

Read biographyClose biography+
Priya Krishnamurthy is co-founder and CEO of CMCA - Children’s Movement for Civic Awareness. The non-profit is focused on building citizenship and life skills in children and youth, with a mission to transform the young into concerned, thinking, and active citizens for an inclusive and sustainable India.

With two decades of experience in program design and developing content and pedagogy, CMCA’s programs are enjoyable and powerful because she is first a passionate practitioner who weaves research-backed learning principles into experiential education.

Ms Nisha Joseph

Principal, St. Anne's Degree College for Women

Ms Nisha Joseph, Principal of St Anne’s Degree College for Women, has two decades of rich teaching experience in the field of Management.

Read biographyClose biography+
Ms Nisha Joseph, Principal of St Anne’s Degree College for Women, has two decades of rich teaching experience in the field of Management. She has double post-graduation in Education and Management from Mount Carmel Institute of Management and has also completed her M.Phil.

She has been instrumental in organizing key institutional programs, seminars, and NAAC accreditation initiatives. She strongly believes in teamwork and fostering values in students which enables them to excel in life and community service.
