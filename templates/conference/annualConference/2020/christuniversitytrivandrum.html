CONFERENCE SCOPE

Community Partnership & Service

The 2020 Trivandrum conference established fruitful dialogues between grassroots non-profit organizations, university scholars, and student volunteers, introducing community service models and intervention pathways to address community challenges during a crucial period.

Featuring keynote dialogues and multi-disciplinary panels in Education, Health, and Social Services, the forum mobilized civic volunteers across South Kerala.

Multi-sector collaboration connecting schools, NGOs, and hospitals
Intergenerational volunteerism programs bridging youth and seniors
Organizational presentations highlighting measurable local outcomes
