CONFERENCE SCOPE

Sustainable Synergies

The conference highlighted key areas including health, education, and social sustainability, emphasizing practical community engagement, wetland conservation, and inter-organizational learning.

Collaborative panels bridging non-profits, student volunteers, and researchers
Multi-stakeholder workshops on sustainable living and ecosystem preservation
Inter-departmental student leadership across 8 specialized working committees
