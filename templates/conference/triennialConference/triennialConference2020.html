FOUNDATIONAL MANDATE

Empowering Communities

OUR MISSION

Creating Inclusive Spaces

The 2020 summit marked the first Triennial International LET US DREAM Conference. Conducted virtually during the global pandemic, it offered an international venue to explore and share varied community-service possibilities, evidence-based intervention models, and intergenerational volunteer opportunities. The primary intent was to create an inclusive, diverse, cross-cultural environment for shared growth through community improvement discourse among scholars, practitioners, and volunteers of all ages.

OUR VISION

Sustainable Global Impact

Empowering local communities for sustainable global impact, continuous improvement, and holistic success.

CONFERENCE GOALS

Key Broad Objectives

The Let Us Dream Conference was established in 2017 to provide a collaborative forum for sharing successes and addressing real challenges:

  • Improve international networking and collaboration among community organizations and academic partners.
  • Promote volunteerism and empower employees, post-secondary students, and practitioners.
  • Establish a professional learning global community rooted in shared knowledge and evidence-based theories.
