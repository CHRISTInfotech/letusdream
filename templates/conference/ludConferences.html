The 4 Pillars of Organizing

Key stages to delivering a memorable community conference

1. Alignment & Date

Secure host university endorsement, assemble your local organizing committee, and lock in conference dates.

2. Curation & Speakers

Design thematic panels across Education, Health, and Social Work while inviting unsung community changemakers.

3. Registration via CMT

Configure your conference portal on CMT, coordinate student participant registrations, and release promotional materials.

4. Execution & Reporting

Manage day-of-event logistics, run live streams, capture feedback, and publish your conference proceedings.
