Academic Leadership Course

Empowering Aspiring Leaders Through Action

The Young Community Leadership Program (YCLP) is an applied, interdisciplinary leadership curriculum designed to equip emerging collegiate leaders with the competencies and ethical compass needed to generate sustainable community impact.

Emphasizing highly experiential coursework, authentic relationship building, and active field engagement, YCLP provides undergraduate students with hands-on practice in mentorship, team coaching, and civic initiative management.

Through structured partnerships with identified regional schools, YCLP participants plan and conduct intensive peer-mentoring circles, transforming theoretical leadership into real-world social uplift.

Hands-On Field Practicum

Three-Year Structured Experiential Learning

YCLP students are trained to identify and sensitively respond to the psycho-social, emotional, and leadership needs of adolescent students in partner schools. Working under faculty guidance, mentors deploy interactive pedagogical modules—integrating training workshops, reflective discussions, sports, music, theater, and arts—across a comprehensive three-year developmental roadmap.

Concurrently, collegiate scholars master the evidence-based DREAMS (Desire, Readiness, Empowerment, Action, Mastery) framework. They learn to draft, implement, and quantitatively evaluate tailored intervention plans that deliver durable, measurable improvements in the academic performance and emotional well-being of young mentees.

Core Pillars

Course Objectives

Four strategic foundational pillars guiding every aspect of the YCLP curriculum.

Leadership & Entrepreneurship

Develop leadership, peer mentoring, organizational management, and entrepreneurial problem-solving skills through community-grounded field projects.

Psycho-Social Assessment

Learn observational frameworks and diagnostic tools to accurately assess psycho-social growth, emotional resilience, and leadership trajectory in adolescents.

Community Collaboration

Formulate empathetic, constructive responses to pressing community issues through multi-stakeholder collaboration with schools, families, and civic groups.

Talent-Driven Mentoring

Channel artistic talents, academic strengths, and sports aptitudes to nurture and elevate underperforming school students' self-belief and leadership acumen.

Graduation Competencies

Course Learning Outcomes

Tangible, demonstrable competencies acquired by every graduate of the YCLP curriculum.

Transformative Mentorship

Applied mentoring and empathetic leadership techniques that effect positive behavioral and academic transformation.

Project Management

Organizing, coordinating, and managing community development initiatives with ethical stewardship and accountability.

Intervention Evaluation

Designing structured community interventions and measuring qualitative and quantitative impact systematically.

Applied DREAMS Mastery

Mastering the 5-stage DREAMS model to address complex societal challenges and inspire youth resilience.

Accreditation & Recognition

Dual Certification Pathways

CHRIST (Deemed to be University) Students

Enrolled undergraduate students from CHRIST (Deemed to be University) receive the official certification of the Young Community Leadership Program (YCLP) alongside their final university graduation diploma, formally recognizing their academic coursework and dedicated field hours.

External Scholars & Global Volunteers

Participating scholars, exchange students, and volunteers from partnering external institutions receive an accredited, verifiable certificate of completion and community leadership credential issued directly by LET US DREAM.
