Uniting Emerging Leaders Across Continents

The Global Youth Leadership Network (GYLN) is an innovative platform connecting student leaders from diverse DREAMS chapters across India, the United States, and Germany. Founded in November 2020, GYLN provides a transformative environment for ethical leadership development, international collaboration, and grassroots community stewardship.
