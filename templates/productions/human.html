Original Anthem

The HUMAN Family Anthem

The HUMAN Family is a heartfelt call urging an urgent awakening to the essence of what it means to be human—embodying Harmony, Unity, Mindfulness, Action, and Nurturing.

Beginning with India’s Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam ("The world is one family"), the composition integrates global philosophies including Africa's Ubuntu ("I am because we are"), Korea's Hongik Ingan, Japan’s Wabi-Sabi, and Sweden’s Lagom.

Global Youth Initiative

The HUMAN Movement

Developing 100,000 Youth Ambassadors for a Sustainable Future

As artificial intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) reshape modern society, the HUMAN movement emerges as a clarion call to restore the essential qualities of humanity that risk becoming eclipsed by rapid technologisation.

The movement features a meticulously designed, one-day workshop guiding collegiate participants through five core stages: Harmony, Unity, Mindfulness, Action, and Nurturing. Youth are empowered to look beyond personal gains and dedicate their passions toward advancing the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Distinguished by its creative integration of live music, theatre, and reflective dialogue, the workshop deeply inspires youth to become 'Steward Leaders' who care for the Earth, their peers, and vulnerable populations.

Our Core Vision

Purpose & Philosophy

This Youth-led workshop nurtures the development of value-based leadership through the 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Incorporating music, dance, theatre, and storytelling, HUMAN aims to engage student leaders from over 100 academic institutions across the globe, empowering them to become SDG Youth Ambassadors and grassroots peer-change agents.
