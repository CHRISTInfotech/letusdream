A Journey of Transforming Dreams into Action

Let Us Dream is a locally rooted and globally established Not-for-Profit Organization conceived with the vision to create holistically developed communities worldwide. Since its inception, we have firmly vouched for the idea of a global family, where each individual and society works interdependently to build a sustainable world.

Global & Inclusive Movement: Composed of passionate individuals and scholars from diverse ethnic groups around the world, united in fostering holistic growth across generations.

Operating through an impactful, evidence-based model, Let Us Dream connects individuals while bridging community organizations together. We nurture and hone untapped potential in psycho-social leadership, volunteerism, and collaborative action.

Empowering Communities & The DREAM Model

The story behind our mission and the scientifically validated psycho-social framework

The Let Us Dream organizational culture is anchored in the universal values championed by St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara — the holistic formation of individuals and their commitment to society. We see humanity as a single global family, where individual progress and collective community wellbeing are deeply interconnected.

Origin of the DREAMS Vision: Father Reverend Lijo Thomas Pathickal, CMI, found his confidence through music and envisioned a program to help shy or underconfident children unlock their hidden potential. The initiative expanded rapidly, reaching a major scientific milestone during his Ph.D. in Educational Leadership at the University of Louisiana, Monroe, guided by Dr. Pauline Leonard.
