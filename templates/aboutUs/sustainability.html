Program Interventions & SDG Alignment

Explore how each core Let Us Dream initiative directly addresses targeted global goals

SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being

This three-year after-school program addresses the holistic health and well-being of each participant left under-attended at their regular schooling, addressing the psychosocial skill development of its stakeholders.

SDG 4 — Quality Education

Differentiating ourselves with an impactful 1:2 mentor-mentee ratio ensures student-centric holistic development. Engaging in diverse interactive sessions bridges formal education with real-world experiences.

SDG 5 — Gender Equality

The program ensures a safe, inclusive environment that fosters equal opportunity and gender parity for all mentors and mentees throughout the intervention.

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

Designed to address education system disparities, the program focuses specifically on underserved students who need mentorship, attention, and growth opportunities to realize their full potential.

SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities & Communities

Equips the upcoming generation with social commitment and civic engagement to actively build, lead, and care for resilient and sustainable communities.

SDG 16 — Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Promotes social harmony, mutual respect, and civic inclusion among stakeholders from diverse socio-cultural backgrounds, cultivating enduring transcultural social institutions.

SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

Accomplishes goals through deep collaboration with higher education universities, public schools, and community organizations, fostering an extensive glocal network.

Scientific Testing & Validation

Academic Research & Sustainable Impact

Let Us Dream stands as a role model for community organizations by integrating a dedicated academic research wing with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Our international research team comprises scholars from leading Higher Education Institutions across India, the United States, and Germany.

The team rigorously tests and validates the effectiveness of all six community interventions through empirical studies, publishing peer-reviewed research tracking longitudinal outcomes across SDGs 3, 4, 5, 10, 11, 13, 16, and 17.
