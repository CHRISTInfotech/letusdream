Evidence-Based Intervention

Fostering Psycho-Social & Leadership Development

The DREAMS (Desire, Readiness, Empowerment, Action and Mastery for Success) model is a structured five-stage approach designed to foster holistic psycho-social and leadership development in young individuals. Grounded in scientific principles, the DREAMS framework initiates with the Desire and Readiness stages—drawing on contemporary goal-setting and motivational theories—while the subsequent Empowerment, Action, and Mastery stages are anchored in self-determination theory (Thomas, L. 2024, Pilot study, DREAMS Program — Journal of School Administration, Research and Development).

Developed by Dr. Fr. Lijo Thomas, this evidence-based, three-year after-school program pairs underperforming middle school students with trained university mentors and seasoned senior adults from local communities. Guided by the core tenets of mentoring, modeling, and motivating, mentors inspire, coach, and empower young participants to cultivate enduring resilience, personal mastery, and academic ambition.

Intergenerational Mentorship

Bridging Generations for Transformative Community Impact

The DREAMS program is an impactful three-year intervention designed to cultivate authentic leadership, emotional intelligence, civic responsibility, and social consciousness. By harnessing the dynamic potential of university student volunteers, DREAMS creates a purposeful synergy across three generations: adolescents, young adults, and senior adults.

A foundational objective is reconnecting local neighborhoods with higher education institutions. Through intercultural and intergenerational mentoring, senior adults (retirees and seasoned volunteers) share accumulated wisdom, life perspective, and values, while collegiate mentors provide technical guidance and academic coaching. This mutual exchange bridges societal divides, reinforces social cohesion, and nurtures a shared sense of civic duty.

Operational Model

Structured Three-Year Mentorship Architecture

The comprehensive three-year curriculum focuses on personal, interpersonal, and leadership development for middle school students, systematically supported by collegiate mentors and senior community partners.

Each cohort unites 40–45 middle school students in need, 30 college mentors, and 8–10 senior volunteers, clustered into focused circles around the D-R-E-A-M framework. A close 1:2 mentoring ratio ensures personalized, empathetic attention for every youth participant.

Framework for Growth

The 5 Stages of the DREAMS Model

A scientifically grounded progression from extrinsic aspiration to intrinsic mastery and sustained lifelong excellence.

D

Desire

External and extrinsic motivations ignite curiosity, fascination, and attraction toward personal and academic life goals.

R

Readiness

Internalizing life goals, transforming extrinsic encouragement into resilient intrinsic commitment and purpose.

E

Empowerment

Equipping participants with essential psycho-social, emotional intelligence, and interpersonal skills for holistic development.

A

Action

Taking proactive, concrete steps toward milestones, building self-efficacy and forward momentum through perseverance.

M

Mastery

Sustained effort culminates in self-mastery and excellence, where the cyclical journey begins anew with higher aspirations.
