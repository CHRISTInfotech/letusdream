Our Research Mission

The Let Us Dream research team is committed to the scientific evaluation of community interventions and their measurable outcomes on participants, peer mentors, and broader communities. Our multidisciplinary international scholars conduct high-quality empirical studies that continually refine program efficacy and inform sustainable development policies.

Active Investigations

Current Research Projects

Studies exploring mentoring dynamics, educational efficacy, and comparative psychological frameworks.

Peer Mentor Experiences Study

Analyzing the subjective growth, empathy cultivation, and civic identity development among collegiate peer mentors actively serving in the DREAMS middle school programs.

Leadership Skill Development Analysis

Investigating quantitative trajectories of communication, problem-solving, and emotional intelligence metrics among student mentors over a full multi-semester cycle.

Comparative Efficacy Study (PhD Research)

A rigorous comparative investigation evaluating the intervention efficacy of the DREAMS Educational Program versus the PERMA PRP framework on adolescent wellbeing.
