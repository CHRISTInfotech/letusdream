ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

The Triennial International Community Conference 2026 brings together educators, researchers, practitioners, students, and community leaders from around the world to explore innovative approaches to community engagement, social transformation, and sustainable development. Through dialogue, collaboration, and shared learning, participants will co-create solutions that strengthen resilient glocal communities and promote meaningful humanity.

PROGRAM TRACKS

Three Days. Three Themes.

One Shared Purpose.

Each day of the conference explores a key dimension of community transformation through dialogue, storytelling, research, and collaborative action.

DAY 1

Education: Rethinking Learning Ecosystems - A GLocal Multigenerational Approach

By addressing the limitations of conventional education we consequently promote deeper learning, creativity, and access. With an emphasis on holistic approaches to educational systems and spaces, discussions will focus on exploring alternative educational structures with a multigenerational lens that considers global reach and local designs.

Non-conventional Education

Spaces

Global Reach

Systemic designs

Creativity

Learning

Access

Holistic Approaches

DAY 2

Health: Building Holistic Health - Local Wisdom meets Digital Reality

The possibility to democratize safe and wholesome solutions to health care issues using digital platforms in a local context is the challenge of our times. The urgency to empower healthcare providers with distribution of knowledge and resources on the ground in order to make healthcare more accessible is the priority for decision makers of today.

Wholesome Health

Accessibility

Physical Care

Digital Health Literacy

Mental Wellbeing

DAY 3

Social: Bridging Heritage and Equity: Empowering Communities Through Indigenous Knowledge for Resilient Futures

Highlights how local knowledge, cultural identity, and equitable systems can address global challenges. It connects grassroots realities with global discourse, fostering inclusive development, preserving heritage, and enabling communities to co-create adaptive, context-driven solutions across diverse social and economic landscapes.

Equity

Heritage
